Our story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: a good neighborhood deserves a bakery where people can slow down, find something fresh, and feel welcome. We are a small team of bakers and hosts who care about the everyday details, from a well-shaped loaf to remembering a familiar face.

We keep our menu rooted in classics and make room for seasonal inspiration. Whether you are picking up breakfast on your commute or planning a family gathering, we are glad to be part of your table.

Our approach

Good ingredients. Neighborly service.

Thoughtful sourcing

We look for dependable ingredients from local and regional suppliers when available, and choose quality staples that let the baking shine.

Made in small batches

Small-batch baking helps us keep favorites fresh and make seasonal treats with care.

A place for everyone

We want every guest to feel comfortable asking a question, finding a favorite, or sharing an idea for a special order.

Meet the team

Our bakers, counter team, and order coordinators work together to make each visit easy and welcoming. Ask us what just came out of the oven or tell us what you are celebrating—we love helping you find the right treat.
